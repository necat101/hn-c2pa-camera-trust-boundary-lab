## Narrow findings

- `valid manifest (10/12) ≠ trusted signer (7/12) ≠ uncompromised capture path (10/12 uncompromised, but 2/12 valid+trusted yet compromised) ≠ truth of the depicted scene (12/12 not established)`. Even the “all-green” case `c01` remains `scene_truth_not_established: true` because provenance cannot rule out staging.
- `c02` and `c11` show the requested boundary: signature valid + signer trusted + capture path explicitly compromised — valid ≠ uncompromised.
- `c03`/`c09`/`c07` show valid signature math without trust (off-list, expired, revoked).
- `c06`/`c10` (display / print re-photography) show an honest cryptographic chain with a depiction as the physical subject — provenance alone cannot establish what was in front of the sensor.
- `c05` and `c12` show conforming-product metadata without scene truth or even without capture-path evidence.
- No case emitted `photo_is_real`, `c2pa_proves_truth`, or `attack_proves_c2pa_broken` (tests enforce this).

This lab does not reproduce Buchanan’s Android exploit, compromise secure hardware, or make claims about any real camera. It models the boundary distinctions as synthetic facts.
